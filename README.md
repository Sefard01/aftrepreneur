# What is Afterpreneur?

**Afterpreneur** *(noun)*

An Afterpreneur is a person who thinks beyond traditional entrepreneurship and focuses on creating long-term impact through systems, technology, knowledge, communities, and legacy.

In simple words:

> An Afterpreneur is someone who looks one step beyond entrepreneurship itself.

While entrepreneurs build businesses, Afterpreneurs think about what comes after the business—its impact, sustainability, contribution, and legacy.

---

# Origin

The term **Afterpreneur** was coined by **Himanshu Singh** in 2026.

The idea first came to him on **May 7, 2026**, while doing household work. During that moment, he started thinking about people who not only build businesses but also think beyond them.

The concept was immediately documented and is being publicly recorded through this repository as part of its digital origin history.

# Official Definition

**Afterpreneur (noun)**

*A person who goes beyond traditional entrepreneurship by creating systems, movements, technologies, communities, or legacies designed to generate long-term impact beyond profit.*
